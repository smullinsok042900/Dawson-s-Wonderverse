# Where to find the family friends

These are friends living around the game. Walk near one to hear hello, then tap the friend or the big paw to play. Use the picture map for Earth places and the rocket to visit planets. Names are descriptive until the family chooses names; Dad Duck keeps the name written on his picture.

Both confirmed pairs of twins live beside the Adventure Trail bridge. The pink-paper twins are on the castle side. The brown long-eared twins are on the other side, before the climbing rocks. Each twin keeps its own shape and expression.
