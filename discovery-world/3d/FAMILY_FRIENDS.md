# Where to find the new family friends

Build 121 adds all sixteen creations as friends living in the world. Use the picture map to visit their district, then walk to the landmark below. Walk near a friend to hear hello; tap the friend or the big paw to play. Names are descriptive until the family chooses names.

The two pink-paper twins live together on the castle side of the Adventure Trail bridge. The two brown long-eared twins live together on the other side, before the climbing rocks. Each twin keeps its own shape and expression.
