# Daughter's chalk friends

The parent asked to turn the two supplied sidewalk drawings into real 3D world characters using the existing game's style and interactions. These are residents to meet, not selectable player characters. The daughter has not named them; use Pink Chalk Friend and Green Chalk Friend as descriptive names.

Preserve the pink drawing's large rounded head, oval eyes, smile with two downward teeth, chunky upright body, long thin rounded legs and long curved tail. Preserve the green drawing's oversized head, projecting angular snout, back spikes, curved lower-face contour and narrow curved body/base. Filled pink/lilac and lime-green surfaces are an implementation interpretation of the chalk outlines. Do not store the private reference photographs in the public repository.

Use the existing painted toy material and one merged mesh per creature, shared geometry, district streaming and the planet-life NPC system. Pink lives in Art Plaza near x827, z-2.7; green in Toy Town near x587, z-4.2. Keep wandering within 1.5 m of home and clear of existing activities. They automatically greet nearby players, face them and respond to tapping or the paw button with speech, sounds and movement. Pink twirls; green copies walking and jumps. Reuse existing reaction fields, with no new timers, shaders or per-frame subsystem.

Alternatives considered: imported GLB models add an asset pipeline and are unnecessary for these rounded creatures; textured flat drawings would not satisfy real 3D. Procedural low-poly shapes match the current NPCs and remain offline. This implementation choice is made by the developer from the user's request.

Keep avatars, pets, saved progress and all attractions intact. Build 120 must have a backup, focused shape/interaction/lifecycle checks, existing unit and browser suites, before/after desktop software-rendering measurements, and honest documentation of device limits. Submit a development-branch PR; the parent merges it. No paid services.
